# How to Change Your Password (Windows)

## What this helps with
Changing your Windows password if it expired or you need to update it.

## Steps
1. Press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete**
2. Select **Change a password**
3. Enter your **current password**
4. Enter your **new password** twice
5. Press **Enter** (or click the arrow) to save

## If you still need help
Contact the Service Desk and include:
- The exact error message (if any)
- Whether you are on-site or working remote/VPN
